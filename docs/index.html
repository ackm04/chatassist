Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

A full-featured chat platform that stays out of your way — lightweight, fast, and built to WordPress standards.

🔌

Plug-and-Play Chat URL

Drop any webhook URL — n8n, AI bot, or custom endpoint — and the widget goes live instantly. Zero coding required.

📋

Pre-Chat Lead Form

Capture name, email, and phone before the chat opens. Data is passed directly to your chat URL for seamless CRM integration.

🎯

Smart Display Rules

Show or hide the widget by page, device, time of day, day of week, user role, or geo location. Surgical targeting, no code.

🛒

WooCommerce-Aware

Target Shop, Product, Cart, Checkout, and Account pages individually. Show a sales widget on pricing, support on docs.

📊

Built-in Analytics

Track opens, closes, messages, and conversions with daily breakdowns. Export reports as PDF or CSV in one click.

🧪

A/B Testing

Split-test different chat experiences. Compare variants side-by-side with built-in conversion stats to pick the winner.

🪟

Multiple Widget Profiles

Run different chat URLs on different pages. Sales widget on /pricing, support on /docs — unlimited profiles.

👁

Live Preview & Zoom

See exactly how your widget looks at any zoom level (50–150%) before saving. What you see is what your visitors get.

🎯

Goal & Conversion Tracking

Track WooCommerce order completions and custom events as conversions. Tie chat engagement directly to revenue.

📣

Marketing Newsletter Modal

Built-in newsletter signup with exit-intent, on-chat-open, and post-message timing. Grows your list automatically.

📱

PWA + Push Notifications

Installable web-app manifest and Web Push support via external VAPID service. Re-engage visitors after they leave.

🔒

GDPR Consent Banner

Built-in consent flow loads before any tracking fires. Fully compliant out of the box — no extra plugin needed.

♿

Accessibility-First

Full keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, and screen-reader-friendly markup. WCAG-conscious from the ground up.

🌍

Fully Translatable

.pot file included. Loads .mo translations from /languages — ready for any language or multilingual plugin.

🔗

REST API

Read-only analytics endpoints with full capability checks. Pull your data into dashboards or headless setups.

📡

Outbound Webhooks

Fire events to Slack, Discord, HubSpot, Salesforce, or any generic JSON endpoint. Keep your stack in sync.

🗺

Heatmap & Scroll Tracking

Visualize scroll depth and click patterns right inside the dashboard. Understand where your visitors engage.

Quick Start

Live in under 60 seconds.

No build steps. No API keys to hunt down. Just activate and paste your URL.

1

Install the Plugin

Search "ChatAssist by Ajay" in WP Admin → Plugins → Add New. One click to install and activate.

2

Paste Your Chat URL

Drop in any webhook URL — your n8n workflow, AI bot endpoint, or third-party service.

3

Pick Your Style

Choose position, color, icon, and zoom. Hit Save. The widget appears on every page instantly.

4

Track & Optimise

Open the Analytics tab, set up display rules, run A/B tests, and connect your webhooks.
